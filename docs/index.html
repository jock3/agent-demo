Why bananas?

Everything you need in a natural peel

The banana is more than just a fruit. It is a smart choice for your whole body – every day.

⚡

Fast energy

Natural carbohydrates that give you power when you need it most.

💪

Rich in potassium

Potassium contributes to normal muscle function and to the maintenance of normal blood pressure.

🌿

Fibre for digestion

A natural source of fibre that helps you feel full for longer.

🎒

Perfect on the go

No preparation, no utensils. Just peel and enjoy – wherever you are.

🧠

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.
